# Release compliance status

See [the current review](license-review-2026-09-29.md) and
[`compliance/release.json`](../compliance/release.json) for current evidence.
The latter is generated by `scripts/collect-compliance.py report`.

Dependency notice coverage and source archive integrity are separate from a
release approval. A successful `compliance:check` does not approve a binary
distribution. Packaging requires an actual source/license review; distribution
additionally requires verification of publicly available matching source.

Do not replace a pending review with an approval just to get a build through.
Keep historical reviews and source records for older distributed binaries.
The September 2026 source-only prereleases do not approve later installers.

The [licensing guide](licensing.txt) describes collection, review, source
publication and inspection of packaged notices. The
[rebuild guide](rebuilding-third-party.txt) describes component reconstruction.
