# Commercial licensing

Yougori LLC offers original Yougori code under two alternative licensing routes:

- **AGPL-3.0-only:** the public license in [LICENSE](LICENSE). No payment to
  Yougori LLC is required to exercise this license. Commercial use, distribution
  and hosting are allowed when the applicable AGPL requirements are met.
- **A separate paid commercial agreement:** individually agreed rights for
  covered Yougori-owned material, for example where a customer needs permission
  for a proprietary integration or modified deployment that they cannot provide
  under the AGPL option.

To request commercial terms, contact Yougori LLC through
[yougori.com](https://yougori.com/), identifying the intended use and versions.
The covered code, license grant, fees, duration, distribution or hosting rights,
and any support or warranty are specified in the written agreement. This page
announces the commercial option; it is not an executed agreement and grants no
additional rights by itself. Purchasing a service or making a payment without
such an agreement does not change the software license.

## Third-party and contributor rights

A commercial agreement can cover only rights Yougori LLC owns or is authorized
to grant. It does not remove GPL, LGPL, MPL, Apache, MIT, BSD or other third-party
conditions. QEMU, its GPL-covered modifications, and separately licensed project
components retain their own terms. Their source, notice, replacement/relinking
and other applicable obligations remain in force.

New original contributions require a separate signed copyright assignment to
Yougori LLC before acceptance. This policy does not transfer ownership of
earlier contributions or third-party components. Any contributions still owned
by others require documented permission for the proposed commercial licensing;
publication under AGPL alone is not such permission.

## Existing licenses

Previously signed commercial agreements keep their applicable terms.
Yougori trademarks are governed separately.

See [the licensing guide](docs/licensing.txt) for source distribution and the
scope of the public license. There is no internal-use-only or non-commercial
restriction added to the AGPL option.
